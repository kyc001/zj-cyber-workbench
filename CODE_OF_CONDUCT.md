# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

Project participants must communicate respectfully, review work on technical merit, welcome constructive disagreement, and protect private or security-sensitive information. Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate disruption, credential disclosure, and unauthorized security testing are not acceptable.

## Security Research

All demonstrations and reproductions must use local, controlled, or explicitly authorized targets. Do not pressure another contributor to share secrets or access, and do not publish an unresolved vulnerability without coordinated disclosure.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the repository maintainers. Maintainers may edit or remove content, reject contributions, restrict participation, or escalate serious threats to the hosting provider or appropriate authorities. Reports will be handled with the minimum disclosure necessary to investigate them.
